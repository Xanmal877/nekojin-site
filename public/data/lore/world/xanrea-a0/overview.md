# Xanrea A0
Xanrea A0 is the primary instance of the world, often referred to as the "Genesis" or "Root" instance.

## Characteristics
- **Stability**: A0 is the most stable of all instances, serving as the baseline for all other derivative clusters.
- **Density**: It contains the highest concentration of original "Traveler" souls.
- **The Root**: The center of A0 is where the server's primary administrative conduits are most accessible.

## A0 Logistics
Because A0 is the root, anomalies here have the potential to ripple across all other clusters. This makes the stability of A0 critical to the continued existence of the entire Xanrea network.
