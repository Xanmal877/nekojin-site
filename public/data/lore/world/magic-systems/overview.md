# Magic Systems
Magic in Xanrea is the manipulation of "Essence" and "Server Logic." What the inhabitants perceive as magic is often the intentional or accidental triggering of server-side functions.

## Essence
Essence is the fundamental energy source for all life and magical abilities.
- **Acquisition**: Essence can be gained through natural growth, specific rituals, or intimate acts (especially for species like Kitsune).
- **Expenditure**: Magic is cast by spending essence to overwrite the local simulation state.

## Elemental Systems
The world utilizes a system of primary elements. These are not just "fire" or "water," but specific data-types that govern how entities interact with the world.
- **The Sorting**: An example of high-level elemental manipulation used by Elves to create new life.
- **Fae Fire**: A specialized form of essence that causes sensory overload (pain) without damaging the underlying data (physical body).

## Soulmancy
The rarest and most dangerous form of magic, soulmancy involves directly editing the "Soul" data of an entity. This is strictly forbidden by the Moderator AIs and can lead to permanent deletion from the server.
