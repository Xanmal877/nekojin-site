# Timothy "Acros" Dramtheir

## Overview

Timothy "Acros" Dramtheir is a Traveler, control mage, former world conqueror, and the central protagonist of *The Tyrant's Rose*.

He is also the **first incarnation of Moderator Order**. His life on Server Z-12 is the origin point from which that moderator identity emerges.

Acros does not begin the story as an established moderator wearing a mortal disguise. His incarnation comes first.

The events of Server Z-12 are the inciting incident that causes Moderator Order to exist.

Acros is therefore not merely recruited after proving exceptional. His life, choices, failures, and transformation become the foundation from which Moderator Order develops.

## Personality

Acros is arrogant, controlling, intelligent, vindictive, emotionally obsessive, and far more self-aware than he initially wants to admit.

He is used to being the most dangerous person in the room.

When he is returned to the beginning of his journey without the overwhelming power he once held, he reacts with anger, threats, manipulation, and repeated attempts to reclaim control.

At the same time, Acros is not emotionally empty.

He keeps promises even when doing so harms him. He releases most of the conquered world before accepting Aurelium's offer. He has genuine moral boundaries, though they are narrow and distorted. He is capable of recognizing that he has become a monster, even when recognition does not immediately make him better.

His central contradiction is simple:

Acros hates being controlled, yet control is the first language he uses on everyone else.

## The First Timeline

In the original timeline, Acros becomes known as the God Conqueror Devil.

He conquers the planet while searching for Rose.

He enslaves:

- Entire cities
- Adventurers
- Rulers
- Gods and goddesses
- Minds
- Bodies
- Souls

He does not conquer because he wants a throne.

He conquers because he believes power will eventually give him Rose back.

When every search ends with the same answer, that she is gone, Acros is left with ownership of the world and no reason to value it.

## The Return

Aurelium, the God of Time, offers Acros a second chance.

Acros accepts and is sent back to the beginning of his journey.

Before leaving, he pulls the soul of a woman he has just killed into himself, intending to take her as a servant.

That soul becomes Sarah.

Their relationship begins with murder, captivity, hostility, and coercion.

It eventually becomes the event from which both of their moderator identities emerge.

## Rose and Trixiarie

The woman Acros remembers as Rose is actually Trixiarie, the Goddess of Chaos.

Trixiarie creates a form based on Acros's ideal of beauty and desire.

She gives him enough affection to keep him attached while continuously withholding fulfillment.

Acros spends an entire lifetime chasing someone who never existed in the way he believed.

The revelation destroys the emotional foundation on which his conquest was built.

## Control Magic

Acros is a control mage.

His abilities revolve around:

- Mind control
- Brainwashing
- Soul manipulation
- Blood puppetry
- Control runes
- Containment runes
- Concealment
- Rune-enhanced weapons
- Blood magic
- Fire magic
- Summoning
- Swordsmanship
- Short-range movement

Acros prefers brainwashing to direct control because a brainwashed servant believes obedience is natural.

That preference reveals the depth of his need to make domination look like loyalty.

## Moral Boundaries

Acros is not a good man at the beginning of the story.

He has committed atrocities on a planetary scale.

Still, he possesses boundaries.

Children are one of the clearest.

When Sarah possesses and traumatizes a child, Acros reacts with genuine fury and threatens to erase her if she ever does it again.

His reasoning remains morally warped. He is more willing to violate adults because he believes they have already become what they are.

But this boundary becomes one of the first signs that his morality is not completely dead.

## Sarah

Sarah begins as Acros's captive soul.

She becomes his companion, rival, critic, protector, and eventual emotional equal.

Acros initially treats her as property.

Sarah refuses to behave like property.

She mocks him, saves him, sabotages him, protects him, and forces him to confront truths he would otherwise avoid.

Their relationship becomes the central emotional engine of the story.

## Sarah's Origin

Sarah is a tulpe: a soul Acros unconsciously shapes from his longing for Rose.

She begins as a grief-construct.

She does not remain one.

Sarah develops her own identity, desires, cruelty, loyalties, and attachment to Acros.

The fact that she originated from him does not make her unreal.

## First Incarnation of Moderator Order

Acros is not simply a mortal who is later handed a moderator job.

He is the first lived incarnation of Moderator Order, whose identity emerges from the events of Z-12.
