# Moderator Space

## Overview

Moderator Space is the system-level moderator responsible for spatial structure and the relationships between locations.

The role governs how places connect, how distance functions, and how entities move through the system.

Moderator Space is distinct from any single incarnation or mortal life.

## Role in the System

Moderator Space is responsible for spatial operations such as:

- Position
- Distance
- Location
- Spatial continuity
- Movement between regions
- Connections between spaces
- Routing across the system
- Preventing incompatible locations from overlapping incorrectly

The exact limits of the role depend on the architecture of the system and the authority available to the moderator.

## Nature of the Position

Moderator Space is a system office as well as an identity.

A being holding the role becomes responsible for maintaining spatial coherence across the system.

The position can continue even when the original soul moves on, provided a valid continuation remains to perform the work.

## Known Holder

### Xanari Telis

Xanari eventually becomes Moderator Space.

A copy of his soul remains behind as the Moderator AI while the original continues onward.

This allows the role to persist without requiring the original Xanari to remain permanently trapped inside the system.

See **[Xanari Telis](Xanari.md)** for his full biography.

## Narrative Significance

Moderator Space represents the cost of becoming essential infrastructure.

The role asks whether continuity of identity depends on the original person remaining present, or whether a true copy can carry responsibility forward without reducing either version to a fraud.
