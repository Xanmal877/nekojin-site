# Fuyuki "Tama" Tamaneko

## Overview

Fuyuki "Tama" Tamaneko is a royal Nekojin, trained ninja, princess of Trissaile, and one of the central protagonists of the story.

She begins her journey as a young adult whose cheerful, childlike manner hides years of trauma, brutal training, and emotional isolation.

Tama is also an incarnation of **Admin Creation**, though she lives much of her mortal life without access to that administrative identity.

## Personality

Tama is energetic, direct, affectionate, impulsive, and fiercely protective.

She often appears simple because she speaks plainly and refers to herself in the third person. In reality, she is more perceptive than she lets on. She notices emotional pain quickly, trusts decisively, and often understands people through instinct rather than careful analysis.

Her optimism is genuine, but it is also armor.

Tama tends to avoid sitting with her own pain by moving, helping others, training, or throwing herself into danger.

## Early Life

Tama was born into Trissaile's royal bloodline, though she grew up away from court.

While she was still a young child, undead forces destroyed her village and killed her parents.

She was later found by Reginald Torweather, whom she came to know as Sensei.

Sensei raised and trained her for years in isolation.

## Sensei

Sensei was Tama's rescuer, teacher, and father figure.

He was also the source of severe psychological harm.

His methods relied on abandonment, overwhelming danger, and survival under extreme pressure. The training made Tama extraordinarily capable, but it also taught her to confuse suffering with growth and cruelty with preparation.

When Tama later finds Sensei transformed into an undead being, she is forced to kill him herself.

Her relationship with his legacy remains one of the central conflicts of her life.

## Third-Person Speech

Tama's habit of referring to herself by name is connected to trauma and emotional distance.

It is not merely a cute speech pattern.

Using "Tama" instead of "I" gives her a small separation from painful memories and feelings she struggles to claim directly.

## Royal Identity

Tama is the legitimate heir to Trissaile.

Her royal blood gives her political legitimacy and access to magically enforced authority.

She can issue decrees, appoint officials, and reshape governance, though she strongly prefers direct action over ceremony or administration.

Her moral instincts are often clear.

Her political solutions are often explosive.

## Abilities

Tama is one of the most dangerous close-range fighters in the story.

Her abilities include:

- Extraordinary speed
- Master-level swordsmanship
- Stealth and infiltration
- Heightened hearing and spatial awareness
- Water magic
- Air magic
- Rapid recovery
- Extreme endurance

Her speed is so great that observers often describe it as blinking or teleportation.

Her signature weapon is **Serenity**, an enchanted katana tied closely to her identity as a ninja.

## Relationship with Saki

Saki is Tama's best friend, royal advisor, and emotional counterpart.

Tama gives Saki trust and acceptance.

Saki gives Tama strategy, restraint, and someone willing to challenge her.

Their friendship is genuine and exists independently of their larger identities as incarnations of Admin Creation and Admin Destruction.

## Relationship with Anna

Anna is an incarnation of Moderator Time and is deeply aligned with Admin Creation.

She often serves as Tama's clothing, armor, healer, companion, and protector.

Their bond is affectionate and real, though it also contains an unavoidable hierarchy.

## Character Arc

Tama's story is about learning that survival is not the same as healing.

She must confront the harm hidden inside Sensei's training, accept that leadership cannot always be reduced to direct action, and learn that protecting people does not require carrying every burden alone.

Her life as Tama ultimately changes how Admin Creation understands creation itself.
