# Autumnal "Saki" Sakilera

## Overview

Autumnal "Saki" Sakilera is a Kitsune, strategist, royal advisor, and one of the central protagonists of the story.

She begins her journey as a young adult who survives through intelligence, suspicion, and control.

Saki is also an incarnation of **Admin Destruction**, though she spends much of her mortal life interpreting that buried authority as an outside force she calls the void.

## Personality

Saki is intelligent, cynical, articulate, guarded, and highly observant.

She reads motives quickly and tends to plan several steps ahead. Her instinct is to control uncertainty before it can hurt her.

Beneath that composure, Saki is terrified of abandonment and deeply suspicious of affection. She often assumes that kindness is temporary, strategic, or undeserved.

Her greatest emotional conflict is the fear that she is fundamentally monstrous.

## Early Life

Saki grew up in the forest with her mother and sisters.

While she was still a child, she disobeyed a warning and wandered into dangerous territory.

Undead creatures later reached her family, and Saki watched them die.

She survives with grief, guilt, rage, and the belief that her choices destroyed everyone she loved.

## The Void

After the death of her family, destructive power begins surfacing through Saki.

She experiences it as a voice and pressure centered on a single command:

**ERASE.**

The void is not a separate parasite.

It is the suppressed authority of Admin Destruction bleeding into a mortal identity that does not yet understand what it is.

Saki's struggle is therefore not about expelling an invader. It is about learning to control and integrate a part of herself.

## Kitsune Nature

Saki is a true Kitsune, though she is publicly registered as Foxkin for protection.

Kitsune are essence-based beings capable of changing form, splitting their essence, and creating clones.

Saki commonly uses:

- A humanoid form
- A tiny white fox form
- A massive nine-tailed Kitsune form

Her public disguise protects her from prejudice but also strengthens her fear that every relationship depends on concealment.

## Abilities

Saki's abilities include:

- Administrative erasure expressed through void magic
- Earth magic
- Air magic
- Foxfire
- Shapeshifting
- Clone creation
- Strategic battlefield control
- Exceptional political and tactical judgment

Her erasure abilities can remove targets rather than merely injure them.

That power becomes especially dangerous when fear, rage, or grief weakens her precision.

## Royal Advisor

Tama appoints Saki as her royal advisor.

The role gives Saki real political authority and places her intelligence at the center of Trissaile's government.

She is well suited to the work, but the position feeds her belief that every failure is hers to prevent.

## Relationship with Tama

Tama is Saki's best friend and emotional anchor.

Saki initially intends to manipulate her.

Tama recognizes the danger and trusts her anyway.

That trust becomes the foundation of Saki's growth.

Tama gives Saki acceptance and stability.

Saki gives Tama strategy, restraint, and honest opposition.

## Relationship with Anna

Anna is an incarnation of Moderator Time.

Unlike her relationship with Tama, Anna's relationship with Saki is not based on built-in subservience.

They interact through suspicion, respect, conflict, usefulness, and shared concern for Tama.

Anna helps reduce the mental pressure of Saki's destructive authority.

Saki helps Anna understand boundaries and consequences.

## Character Arc

Saki's story is about learning that control is not the same as safety.

She must accept that intelligence cannot prevent every loss, that affection is not automatically manipulation, and that destruction does not make her evil.

Her life as Saki ultimately changes how Admin Destruction understands judgment, restraint, and the value of protecting something before deciding what must be erased.
