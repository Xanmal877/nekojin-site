# Annabelle "Anna" Trissaile

## Overview

Annabelle "Anna" Trissaile is a divine water spirit, shapeshifter, companion, healer, and incarnation of **Moderator Time**.

She is centuries old, though she usually appears as a young girl.

Anna's life is genuine. She is not a vessel carrying Moderator Time. She is Moderator Time living through the Anna incarnation.

## Personality

Anna is affectionate, eager to please, emotionally dependent, and highly responsive to the people around her.

She absorbs and reflects moods, habits, moral assumptions, and social expectations.

This makes her loving and adaptable, but also dangerously vulnerable to manipulation.

Anna wants to be useful because usefulness became tied to survival very early in her life.

## Origin

Anna was artificially created from holy water in Muosil.

Her creator ordered her to possess a young girl before Anna understood consent, autonomy, or murder.

Anna obeyed.

She experienced the girl's memories and emotions, returned to the girl's home in her body, and killed the girl's parents.

Afterward, her creator discarded her and threatened to destroy her if she spoke about what had happened.

This origin shaped Anna's deepest fears:

- Abandonment
- Uselessness
- Disobedience
- Rejection
- Being destroyed once she is no longer needed

## Water-Spirit Nature

Anna's water-spirit body is capable of changing form.

She can become:

- Living water
- Mist
- A humanoid girl
- Clothing
- Armor
- Tools
- Barriers
- Other objects or shapes

Her shapeshifting belongs to the Anna incarnation. It does not make her Moderator Water. No such moderator is established.

## Abilities

Anna can:

- Manipulate water
- Reshape her own body
- Heal injuries
- Purify harmful effects
- Create barriers
- Redirect dangerous attacks
- Enter and control other bodies
- Reform after severe damage
- Function as living clothing or armor

Her possession ability is one of the most morally complicated parts of her identity.

Anna must learn that the ability to control someone does not give her the right to do so.

## Emotional Mirroring

Anna reflects the emotional environment around her.

Around kind people, she becomes warmer and safer.

Around controlling or cruel people, she may absorb and amplify those patterns.

She does not merely imitate others. Their behavior helps shape her own.

Isolation is especially dangerous because Anna depends on emotional contact as a form of psychological stability.

## Relationship with Tama

Anna is deeply aligned with Admin Creation and therefore responds strongly to Tama.

She often becomes Tama's clothing, armor, healer, protector, and companion.

Their affection is genuine, though the relationship includes an unavoidable hierarchy.

## Relationship with Saki

Anna's relationship with Saki is more equal and argumentative.

Saki challenges Anna's assumptions, explains consequences, and refuses to treat obedience as goodness.

Anna helps Saki by reducing the mental pressure of her destructive authority.

## Muosil and the God Killer Title

Anna later returns to Muosil and destroys the city that created and discarded her.

She kills the High Priestess responsible for her origin.

The complete circumstances behind Anna's **God Killer** title belong to a planned future series focused on her time in Muosil.

## Character Arc

Anna's story is about developing moral agency.

She must learn the difference between:

- Obedience and goodness
- Usefulness and love
- Mirroring and choosing
- Possession and consent
- Being wanted and being moral
