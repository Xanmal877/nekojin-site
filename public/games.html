From the first page of the Guide

Saki is a fox-eared adventurer raised by kitsune, and she lives in the village of Trelmar. She eats, she drinks, she sleeps in. She takes long strolls she insists are scouting. And when she decides it's time, she heads into the Forest Den, fights whatever lives there, loots whatever isn't nailed down, and walks home to tell everyone about it.

You do not control her. There is no Delve button and no order queue. You watch, you read why she made each choice, and you spend Influence on small gifts she is free to use, save, or completely ignore. Every run begins with one of her decisions.

  • 🦊She decidesGoals, routes, targets and when to go home are all hers.
  • 🎁Gifts, not ordersInfluence buys help she can accept. Or sell. Or eat.
  • 🏡A life between runsTrelmar keeps her busy with meals, naps and village life.
  • 📖Read her mindEvery choice is explained, entry by entry, like a guidebook.

The war for Xanrea

A classic real-time strategy game with RPG heroes at its heart. Gather gold and lumber, raise your town hall through its tiers, and field the armies of Trissaile or the Empire, each with its own units, buildings and heroes.

Heroes aren't stat blocks. Every hire is an individual with a name and a personality who levels up, carries items, leads armies and makes their own calls on the field. The computer plays the same way you do: from what it owns and what it can actually see. No scripted build orders. No cheating clocks.

  • ⚔️Heroes with mindsThey level, gear up, lead armies and act on their own.
  • 🏰Two peoplesTrissaile and the Empire play nothing alike.
  • 🗺️Skirmish, campaign, editorHand-made maps, story missions, and a map editor of your own.
  • 🌐Play togetherUp to four seats, teams or free-for-all, over LAN.

The premise

Echo World is an attempt to simulate what it would actually be like to live in Xanrea, starting with one bounded corner of it: the Caldera.

The people who live there are Natives. They have lives, goals, work, friendships, rivalries and dangers of their own. Real players enter that same world as Travelers, and the world doesn't stop to wait for them. Leave, come back later, and you return to a world that kept going, not a save file that paused.

  • 🧑‍🌾Natives with livesWork, rest, relationships and ambitions. Not set dressing.
  • 🧳You are a TravelerReal players arrive in a world that was already there.
  • 🌋The CalderaOne small piece of Xanrea, made to convincingly live.
  • ⏳It never pausesEvents happen whether anyone is watching or not.
