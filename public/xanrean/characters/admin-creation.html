True Nature

At the absolute top of the Xanrea system, Admin Creation possesses the authority to create, modify, and restructure anything in the system. The being often takes the form of Tama—a 5-foot-2 nekojin with purple hair, deep sapphire blue eyes, cat ears, and a tail.

In her true adult form, Admin Creation stands over six feet tall with brilliant blonde hair and golden eyes that shift red when genuinely furious. Tama is not a separate being from Admin Creation, but one of the forms Admin Creation took and still sometimes assumes.

Her admin memories were deliberately suppressed so she could experience the world as a person, including its suffering and growth. She can lock entities into what she calls "No-No boxes"—divine-tier system quarantine.

Origin & Tragedy

At age 6, Tama lived peacefully in a small village with her parents. Her mother was secretly royalty from Trissaile who had abandoned her kingdom to live as a peasant. When undead forces attacked, Tama witnessed the destruction firsthand—the most traumatic moment came when "Mommy screamed, and she fell on top of me. It hurt. I saw my mommy's dead eyes staring back at me."

Orphaned and adopted by the legendary "Sensei"—the Sword Master Ninja considered the greatest swordsman to ever live—Tama was raised in complete isolation in the dangerous Trelloark Forest for 10 years. His training methods were brutally effective but psychologically devastating: being kidnapped by goblin tribes, fighting wolf packs naked, constant combat that left "chips in the wood and bits of blood."

When Tama returned to check on Sensei, she found him turned undead and was forced to kill her father figure herself. This devastating loss nearly drove her to suicide until a vision of Sensei stopped her.

Combat Abilities

"Blinking" Speed

Moves so fast others think she's teleporting. Can appear behind enemies, chain multiple blinks, and end fights before opponents react. Visual effect described as "flash of light, flickering flame, explosion."

Serenity

An ornate katana with water-like enchantments that flow like "playful whirlpools of energy." So heavy others can't lift it, yet Tama wields it effortlessly.

Water Mastery

Creates chains, barriers, healing applications; can manipulate existing water sources. Combined with air manipulation for complex battle strategies.

Ninja Training

Trained in legendary sword techniques. Can make blade "ephemeral" to pass through defenses. Enhanced senses—can track by sound alone from 50+ feet.

Royal Decree

As Princess of Trissaile, her commands magically compel obedience. Can claim cities and change governance through royal authority.

Regeneration

Can recover from severe injuries quickly and even regrow severed limbs (though painful).

Psychological Profile

Tama speaks in the third person—a protection from fully acknowledging traumatic experiences. Beneath the childlike exterior lies someone far more perceptive than she appears. She often "let on far less than she actually knew."

Core traits include protective instinct, strong moral compass, emotional honesty, and absolute loyalty once trust is established. Her greatest fear is losing those she cares about, and she struggles with identity confusion between being a ninja and royal responsibilities.

Key Relationships

Adventurer Name
