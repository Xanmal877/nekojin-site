🛡️ The Moderators

The Moderators

System-level moderators with defined responsibilities in the operation of the wider infrastructure. Not merely local gods—they are fundamental system domains.

Below the Admins are moderators responsible for fundamental system operations. Pantheons that emerge inside individual worlds may be extremely powerful within their own environment, but possess no administrative authority compared to these system-level moderators.

The Moderators

Moderator Time

Major Moderator / Time Domain

Responsible for Time itself. Manages the soul stream and functions as the system's CPU-like processor, coordinating timing, routing, continuity, and sequence of soul activity throughout the system.

Xanari "Xan" Telis

Minor Moderator / Former Traveler

A Traveler whose Earth name is unknown. After arriving in Xanrea, he renamed himself Xanari, and others later shortened it to Xan. His eventual ascension to Moderator of Space established that the system can promote a mortal into the administrative hierarchy.

A copy of his soul remains as the Moderator while the original moved on.
