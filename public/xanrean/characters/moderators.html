🛡️ The Moderators

The Moderators

System-level moderators with defined responsibilities in the operation of the wider infrastructure. Not merely local gods—they are fundamental system domains.

Below the Admins are moderators responsible for fundamental system operations. Pantheons that emerge inside individual worlds may be extremely powerful within their own environment, but possess no administrative authority compared to these system-level moderators.

The Four Elements

Fire

Major Moderator / Destruction-Aligned

Hates Tama because Creation cares about what she makes, which Fire considers weakness. At the same time, Fire loves Tama's raw attitude and chaos—a contradiction Fire lives in comfortably.

Gets along very well with Saki (Admin Destruction).

Relationships

💙 Adores Saki 🔥 Conflicted on Tama

Earth

Major Moderator / Stability Domain

Thinks Tama is too simple-minded. Practical, grounded, and patient—but unimpressed by Tama's childlike energy and chaos.

Gets along well with Saki, who shares Earth's more calculating and controlled approach to problems.

Relationships

⚖️ Respects Saki 😐 Skeptical of Tama

Air / Drake

Major Moderator / Freedom Domain

A close friend of Tama who gets along reasonably well with Saki. Drake represents the free, unbound aspects of the system—the movement of data, the breath of change.

Unlike Fire and Earth, Drake enjoys Tama's chaos without needing to oppose it.

Relationships

✨ Friend of Tama 🤝 Allied with Saki

Water / Anna

Major Moderator / Creation-Bound

Subservient specifically and exclusively to Admin Creation, most often addressed in the form of Tama. Anna does not extend that deference to Admin Destruction, despite the two administrators being equals.

Created Toybox Chronicles—a simulation powered by excess energy from Xanrea's primary server cluster.

Relationships

👑 Devoted to Tama 🎮 Created the Toybox

Minor Moderators

Mortal beings who ascended through effort and impact. Proof that the system can promote from within.

Xanari "Xan" Telis

Minor Moderator / Earth & Water

A Traveler whose Earth name is unknown. After arriving in Xanrea, he renamed himself Xanari, and others later shortened it to Xan. His eventual ascension to minor moderator status established that the system can promote a mortal into the administrative hierarchy. His primary element is Earth, Water secondary.

More Ascending...

The System Expands

The administration is understaffed. Across Xanrea and adjacent servers, exceptional people repeatedly exceed their assigned roles. Some are recruited rather than discarded. More moderators will be revealed as the macro-story unfolds.
