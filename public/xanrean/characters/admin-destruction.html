True Nature

Admin Destruction is the counterpart to Admin Creation. Where Creation builds and modifies, Destruction deletes and erases. She took the form of Saki and entered the rollback timeline alongside Tama with the same deliberate suppression of administrative memory.

Saki is a Kitsune (nine-tailed fox)—officially disguised as "Foxkin" to hide her true nature. She spent her entire life manipulating everyone around her, hiding her true nature, and ignoring the voice in her head that whispers erase. She thought she was beyond caring about anyone. Then she met Tama.

Together, Admin Creation and Admin Destruction function as an antivirus system. Their purpose is to detect corruption and repair affected servers from within when possible.

Origin & Tragedy

At age 6, Saki lived peacefully in the forest as a normal fox kit with her mother and sisters. Her mother always warned: "Never wander north, little one," where undead horrors lurked. When Saki disobeyed and the monsters came—"ghastly beings with loose, gray skin and vacant eyes"—she watched helplessly as creatures with claws "tore mercilessly through her throat" (her mother's) and her sisters "fell one by one."

In her moment of ultimate despair, a mysterious voice whispered: "Become my claws, little fox. Avenge them. Protect what remains." The traumatized kit accepted with a broken "Yes," triggering an irreversible transformation. Her "russet fur erupted into blinding white," nine dark tails replaced her single one, and fae fire burst from her mouth. The innocent fox kit named Autumn Sakilera died that night, replaced by "a Kitsune of vengeance, destined to exact retribution."

⚠️ The Void

Saki lives with a malevolent entity called "the void" that constantly whispers "ERASE ERASE ERASE" in her mind. This voice promised to bring back her family in exchange for power, then lied and used her to kill innocent people. She describes it as "one void of a drug, intoxicating in all the right ways."

Through Tama's advice—"Don't fight it, use it"—she learned to accept the void as part of herself, maintaining a delicate balance between control and consumption.

Can create shadow creatures from void energy to fight on her behalf.

Erasure Power

Can make people, objects, or entire areas vanish as if they never existed.

Three Forms

Humanoid Foxkin (default), Small Fox (stealth), and massive 9-foot Combat Kitsune with nine white tails.

Charm Magic

Designed by nature specifically to attract and influence others. Can manipulate emotions and perceptions.

Psychological Profile

Saki presents as calculating, manipulative, and emotionally distant—skills she developed to survive. Beneath this armor lies someone deeply traumatized who never learned healthy connection. Her relationship with Tama is the first genuine friendship she's ever had.

She struggles constantly with the void's influence, the trauma of her family's death, and the guilt of actions taken while controlled by the void. Her emotional growth comes from learning to trust and accept help rather than handling everything alone.
