Moderator Space

The precreated Moderator office responsible for Space, distance, location, boundaries, and the relationships between places throughout the Xanrean Server Cluster.

Overview

Moderator Space is one of the ten precreated Moderator offices within the Xanrean Server Cluster.

Unlike Moderator Time, Space was not one of the original system-created artificial intelligences. The office already existed, but it required a suitable holder selected by an Administrator.

Admin Creation, through Tama, assigned the office to an authorized soul-data copy of Xanari “Xan” Telis.

Space is not emptiness. It is the structure that allows one place to exist apart from another, and still remain reachable.

Role in the System

Moderator Space governs the spatial relationships that allow worlds, locations, bodies, and systems to exist in relation to one another.

The complete technical limits of the office have not yet been fully revealed, but its domain includes the structure of Space itself rather than ordinary movement magic alone.

Distance Governs the separation between places, objects, and regions.
Location Maintains where things exist within spatial systems.
Boundaries Defines the edges between regions, worlds, and contained spaces.
Connection Supports the relationships that make travel between places possible.
Spatial Structure Preserves the geometry and arrangement of environments.
World Navigation Relates distant locations across the cluster without collapsing them together.

Origin of the Office

Moderator Space is a precreated office. The role existed before its current holder and did not emerge from Xanari's personality or life.

Xanari's experiences explain why Tama considered him suitable for the office, but they did not create the title, define its name, or transform him into an unrelated new artificial intelligence.

There is no separate “Moderator AI” associated with Space. Earlier references to one are incorrect.

Assignment by Admin Creation

Tama, acting as Admin Creation, selected the authorized soul-data copy of Xanari “Xan” Telis and assigned him to Moderator Space.

The original Xanari was a Traveler soul. Traveler souls cannot remain permanently within the cluster. He eventually died and passed on.

With his permission, a data-copy of his soul was created before his departure. That copy became the holder of Moderator Space.

The Original and the Copy

The Moderator Space holder looks, acts, thinks, and feels like Xanari. He possesses Xanari's personality, memories, emotional patterns, and relationships.

He also knows that he is not the original Traveler soul.

This distinction is central to his identity. The original Xanari lived, died, and passed on. The copy continues from his preserved soul-data, but does not erase or overwrite the original person's death.

Moderator Space is therefore neither a replacement soul pretending to be Xanari nor an unrelated machine wearing his face. He is an approved continuation created from Xanari's data, with full awareness of the boundary between continuity and identity.

Personality

The current holder retains Xanari's personality rather than becoming an emotionless embodiment of geometry.

His relationships, loyalties, fears, habits, and sense of self continue to matter after the assignment. The office expands what he is capable of doing, but does not dissolve the person chosen to hold it.

The tension at the heart of his branch is not whether the copy is convincing enough to fool others. It is whether a person who knows he is a copy can still claim his own life without pretending the original never died.

Relationship with Admin Creation

Moderator Space owes his office directly to Tama and Admin Creation.

Tama did not create the role for him, but she chose him as its holder and authorized the continuation that made the assignment possible.

Their relationship therefore combines administrative authority, personal trust, and the unusual responsibility of one being deciding that another person's preserved identity may continue after the original soul has departed.

Relationship with Moderator Time

Space and Time are separate offices, but their responsibilities naturally intersect.

Space governs where things are and how places relate. Time governs sequence, continuity, synchronization, and the soul stream. Movement, travel, transfer, and world-to-world transitions depend upon both offices functioning coherently.

The precise working relationship between Moderator Space and Anna or Sabi has not yet been fully revealed.

Narrative Significance

Moderator Space explores continuity across separation.

His story asks whether identity can survive when the original soul is gone, whether memory and personality are enough to create a legitimate continuation, and whether knowing that one is a copy makes a life less real.

The office also embodies the idea that distance is not merely an obstacle. Space is what allows boundaries, individuality, travel, and connection to exist at all.
