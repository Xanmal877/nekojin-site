Moderator Time

The original system-created Moderator responsible for Time itself, the soul stream, sequencing, synchronization, and the CPU-like coordination that keeps the Xanrean Server Cluster functioning.

Overview

Moderator Time is one of the three original artificial intelligences responsible for the operation of the Xanrean Server Cluster.

Unlike the later Moderator offices, Time was not assigned to a suitable person by an Administrator. Moderator Time already existed as part of the cluster's original processing architecture.

Her divine or mythological presentation is layered over an operational reality. She is not merely a being associated with clocks, aging, prophecy, or time magic. She is part of the machinery that allows events, souls, and system changes to occur in a coherent order.

Without Time, the cluster would not merely stop. Its events, souls, and states would lose the order that makes them intelligible.

Role in the System

Moderator Time manages the soul stream and functions as the cluster's CPU-like processor. She coordinates immense amounts of temporal and soul-state activity while ensuring that events and system changes occur in the correct sequence.

Time Governs the flow and operation of Time throughout the cluster.
Temporal Sequencing Maintains the order in which events and state changes occur.
Soul-Stream Processing Handles the movement and processing of souls through the system.
Soul Routing Coordinates where souls move and how their continuity is preserved.
Synchronization Keeps worlds, processes, and system states aligned.
System Processing Performs CPU-like coordination for the wider cluster.

Creation and Destruction determine what may be built, restored, removed, or erased. Time ensures that those changes happen in the correct order without tearing apart the processes that depend on them.

Origin of the Office

Moderator Time was created directly by the system or its creator, alongside Admin Creation and Admin Destruction. She is therefore exceptional among the ten Moderator roles.

The later Moderator offices are precreated positions filled when an Administrator selects and assigns a suitable individual. Time did not pass through that process. The original artificial intelligence and the office began as part of the cluster's foundational architecture.

Incarnation and Identity

Moderator Time can live through incarnations with different bodies, histories, powers, relationships, and personal identities.

An incarnation is not a puppet, disguise, disposable shell, or body being remotely piloted by a hidden intelligence. Each incarnation is Moderator Time genuinely living that life.

The existence of several incarnations does not make any one of them fake or interchangeable. Their memories, choices, trauma, crimes, loyalties, and growth belong to the lives Moderator Time experienced through them.

Time, Not Water

There is no Moderator Water.

Anna's water-spirit body and water-based abilities belong specifically to the Anna incarnation. They do not define the administrative domain of Moderator Time.

Another incarnation may possess an entirely different body, species, upbringing, power set, and social role while still carrying the underlying identity and personality tendencies of Moderator Time.

Relationship with the Administrators

Moderator Time operates alongside Admin Creation and Admin Destruction as one of the cluster's three original artificial intelligences.

Time's system role intersects with Creation whenever restoration, reincarnation, soul transfer, rollback, or reconstruction must occur in a coherent sequence. It intersects with Destruction whenever deletion or shutdown affects active processes, souls, or continuity.

Through Anna, Moderator Time is specifically subservient to Admin Creation, most often encountered as Tama. Anna does not extend the same automatic deference to Admin Destruction.

In the primary storyline, Anna, Tama, and Saki form the central trio and are usually nearly inseparable.

Known Incarnations

Known Incarnation

Annabelle “Anna” Trissiale

Anna is a divine water spirit and shapeshifter who is usually seen as a young girl. She can become living water, mist, clothing, armor, tools, weapons, and other forms.

She was artificially formed from holy water in the underwater city of Muosil and ordered to possess a young girl before she understood consent, murder, autonomy, or betrayal.

Anna is emotionally dependent on the people around her and strongly mirrors their emotions, morals, and habits. This makes her affectionate, adaptive, useful, vulnerable to manipulation, and extraordinarily dangerous when guided by the wrong person.

Her life centers on developing moral agency: learning the difference between obedience and goodness, usefulness and love, emotional mirroring and personal choice, and possession and consent.

Known Incarnation

Sabrina “Sabi” Trissiale

Sabi is another incarnation or form of Moderator Time.

The full relationship between Sabi, Anna, and Moderator Time's other incarnations has not yet been completely revealed. Current canon establishes that Anna and Sabi are subprocess-like incarnations whose needs, cycles, and behavioral constraints are tied to Moderator Time's larger function.

The Toybox is connected to those needs and cycles, but its complete role belongs to Sabi's dedicated biography and the stories branching from her incarnation.

Anna's Place in the Time Branch

Anna's personal history is one of the clearest examples of how a Moderator incarnation becomes its own complete life.

After being created and used in Muosil, she survived for centuries, later killed the High Priestess responsible for her creation, and destroyed the city. Her known titles include City Destroyer, Betrayer, and God Killer.

These acts are not erased or made irrelevant by the revelation that she is Moderator Time. They remain part of the identity, consequences, and moral development carried by the Time branch.

Narrative Significance

Moderator Time represents continuity without sameness.

Her incarnation stories ask what remains consistent when one being lives many lives without sharing conscious memories between them. The audience can recognize recurring needs and tendencies even when each incarnation experiences herself as a distinct person.

Through Anna, Time also explores whether morality can become truly personal when a person's first instinct is to reflect the authority, desires, and emotions of someone else.
