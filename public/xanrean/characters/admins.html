👑 The Admins

The Administrators

At the absolute top of the Xanrea system. Two administrators who function more like an IT department than a pantheon of gods.

The divine hierarchy functions as infrastructure management. The Admins detect corruption and repair affected servers from within. Destruction of damaged server clusters and reconstruction from scratch remains the last resort—but they chose to enter the world as mortals instead.

Admin Creation

The Architect / Tama

Authority to create, modify, and restructure anything in the system. Often takes the form of Tama—a nekojin with purple hair, blue eyes, and a sword named Serenity.

In her true form, Admin Creation stands over six feet tall with brilliant blonde hair and golden eyes that shift red when genuinely furious. Tama is not a separate being, but one of the forms Admin Creation took and still sometimes assumes.

Her admin memories were deliberately suppressed so she could experience the world as a person, including its suffering and growth. She can lock entities into "No-No boxes"—divine-tier system quarantine.

Authority

Admin Destruction

The Rollback / Saki

Where Creation builds and modifies, Destruction deletes and erases. Admin Destruction took the form of Saki and entered the rollback timeline alongside Tama with the same deliberate suppression of administrative memory.

A kitsune who spent her entire life hiding her true nature and ignoring the voice in her head that whispers erase. She thought she was beyond caring about anyone—then she met Tama.

Together, the two administrators function as an antivirus system. Their purpose is to detect corruption and repair affected servers from within when possible.
